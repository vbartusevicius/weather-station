🧭 Calibration idle

Set the station location and time in Setup first: the sun-based steps need them.

1Compass spinbench · 2 minnever

Removes the station's own magnetic bias from the compass.

  • Assembled station, powered
  • Wooden or plastic table
  • 1 m from steel, magnets and phones
  1. Once: find the N barrel (picture below) and mark it outside with a paint dot.
  2. Press Start, then turn the station by hand: 2 slow turns in 90 s.
  3. When Directions covered shows 12 / 12 and the time is up, press Apply result.
Finding the N barrel

Look at the crown board from above. Its one big cut corner carries a silk N ↑. Turn it so that corner is top-right: the transducer barrel at the top is N. Its cable plugs into J2, labelled N. The four posts on the diagonals are struts, not transducers.

2North referencemounted · 5 minnever

Tells the station which way its N mark points. Pick one method; all three give the true bearing of the N mark.

  1. Choose the method and fill in its field.
  2. Tick Fixed heading if the station never turns on its mast (best on steel masts): the compass is then ignored.
  3. Press Start, keep the station still for 15 s, then Apply result.

3Wind zeroindoors · 25 minnever

Makes still air read 0.0 m/s.

  • Cardboard box ≥ 40 × 40 × 40 cm
  • Room without drafts, fans or open windows
  • Station powered ≥ 30 min
  1. Put the box over the station, not touching the transducers.
  2. Leave the room for 10 min.
  3. Press Start and stay away for 60 s. A good result is saved automatically.

4Level the mastmounted · 5 minnever

A level station aims its wind beams at the reflector centre. Target below 1°.

  • Spirit level or a phone level app
  • Mast shims if needed
  1. Watch Tilt in Station health above.
  2. Straighten or shim the mast until it is below 1°.

5Wind speedoutdoors · 30–60 minnever

Compares the station with a known air speed. Pick a method; do at least two runs.

  • Phone hotspot + GPS speed app
  • Power: car 12 V socket, or a USB-C power bank with a 12 V trigger cable
  1. Get a steady speed (or flow) first, then press Start run.
  2. After 30–60 s enter the reference speed and press Finish run.
  3. Repeat in the other direction (A, B) or at a second fan speed (C), then Apply result.

6Pressureanywhere · 2 minnever

Sea-level pressure needs the barometer's exact elevation.

  1. Choose A or B and fill in its fields.
  2. Press Start (B measures for 60 s), then Apply result.

7Light covermounted · 3 minnever

Measures how much light the PTFE dome really passes (about 11 %).

  • Cloudless, haze-free sky
  • Sun ≥ 25° high (see Sun above)
  • Clean, dry dome
  1. Press Start (2 min). Light varies must stay below 4 %.
  2. Press Apply result. Repeating on 2–3 clear days improves it (±10–15 %).

8UV indexmounted · 2 minnever

Optional fine-tuning: the UV-B sensor is factory calibrated; this corrects it for the dome.

  • Sunny, cloudless sky
  • UV index ≥ 1 (A) or ≥ 3 (B, C)
  1. Choose the method; for B or C enter the UV index.
  2. Press Start (60 s), then Apply result.
A · No reference ±15–20 %

Uses the station's second UV sensor (LTR-390, factory calibrated) through the dome. Do Light cover first: it supplies the dome transmission.

B · TEMIS forecast ±10 %

temis.nl gives the clear-sky UV index at solar noon for your coordinates. Run within 15 min of Solar noon (live bar) on a cloudless day.

C · UV meter ±5–10 %

Hold a UV-index meter level next to the dome, in full sun, and enter its reading.

9Rain dropsindoors · 15 minnever

Teaches the rain sensor how hard a drop of known size hits.

  • 5–10 ml syringe, no needle
  • Cup of water
  • Tape measure, chair
  • Towel
  1. Count drops in 1 ml (usually 15–25). Do it twice, enter the average.
  2. Hold the syringe tip 1.5 m above the lid.
  3. Press Start. Drop 20–30 single drops on the flat lid ring, one every 2–3 s.
  4. Press Finish & compute, then Apply result.

10Rain amountoutdoors · during rainnever

Matches the rain total to a simple gauge. Do Rain drops first.

  • Cheap rain gauge, or a straight-walled round container + kitchen scale
  • Open spot, ≥ 2 m from walls and trees
  1. Empty the gauge. Press Start before the rain (a reboot cancels it).
  2. After ≥ 5 mm: enter the gauge mm, or the container diameter and the water weight.
  3. Press Finish & compute, then Apply result.

11Humidity (salt jar)bench · overnightnever

Air over wet salt in a closed box is always 75.3–75.7 % RH.

  • 15 g table salt (1 level tablespoon)
  • 5 ml water (1 teaspoon)
  • Egg cup or shot glass
  • Airtight food box ≈ 1 L
  • Tape or putty
  • Probe board on its cable
  1. Stir the salt and water in the cup into wet sand. Dry crystals must remain; add salt if all of it dissolves.
  2. Put the cup and the probe board in the box, not touching. Seal the cable gap.
  3. Leave 6–12 h (overnight) at steady room temperature, out of the sun.
  4. Press Start (10 min), then Apply result.

12Temperature (ice bath)bench · 40 minnever

Melting ice is exactly 0.0 °C. Optional: the sensor is factory calibrated to ±0.2 °C.

  • Bowl ≥ 1 L full of crushed ice
  • Cold water, just below the ice top
  • 2 zip bags
  • Probe board on its cable
  1. Bag the probe board twice; keep the openings above the water.
  2. Push it ≥ 5 cm under, surrounded by ice. Wait 20 min, stirring now and then.
  3. Press Start (5 min), then Apply result. Water in the bag: unplug and dry the board.

13CO₂ (fresh air)mounted · 5 minnever

Recalibrates the CO₂ sensor to outdoor air (about 425 ppm).

  • Station outdoors, some wind
  • No chimneys, traffic or people nearby
  1. Stay ≥ 3 m away. Press Start (3 min); readings must be steady (±20 ppm).
  2. Press Apply result.

14Self-testsany timenever

Checks for the parts that need no calibration: heater, lightning detector, vibration sensor.

  1. Heater check: after about 10 s De-icing heater in Station health above shows check-pass.
  2. Lightning antenna: the crown tunes it itself at start-up; Lightning in Station health must show 482–518 kHz. Outside that range check the antenna coil and its two 470 pF capacitors.
  3. Lightning interference (no storm within 100 km): disturbers per 10 min should stay low. Press Bursts off for 30 min, then Bursts on: with bursts on, at most twice as many.
  4. During a thunderstorm: compare strikes and distance with the free map at lightningmaps.org. The detector estimates distance in steps (±30 %), it cannot be calibrated.
  5. Vibration / seismic: tap the mast firmly with a knuckle: Vibration in Station health jumps for a moment. The accelerometer is factory calibrated; nothing to adjust.

⬆ Firmware update ready

One file updates everything: the station firmware carries the crown firmware inside it, and the station installs it on the crown by itself after restarting. The update is checked before it is used; a firmware that does not run normally for a minute is undone at the next restart. Keep the power on.

How to read it

115200 baud, 8N1, two wires: terminal 3 = A+ (white), 4 = B− (yellow). The station only answers; send one command per line ending in \n: STATE?, PING, RESET_RAIN, HEAT_CHECK. With an address, prefix it: 7:STATE?; the reply then starts $AM7,.

Reply: $AM,<fields>*<CRC>. The fields are in the order of the table above; an empty field means no valid value. CRC = CRC-16/MODBUS (4 upper-case hex digits) of everything before the *, including the $. Fit the 120 Ω terminator R26 only on the two stations at the ends of a long bus; with more than two stations, keep the 390 Ω bias resistors on one station only.
