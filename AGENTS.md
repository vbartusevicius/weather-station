# Roof Weather Station (AeroMet V1)

## Project intent and current scope

Design and build a solid-state, cylindrical rooftop weather and air-quality station (`Ø 96 × 236 mm` in UV-stabilized ASA) with full end-to-end automated hardware, mechanical, and firmware verification:

- **Power & Dual-Path Connectivity (`main` PCB):**
  - Wide-input **12–32 V DC wired supply + RS-485** on a single 4-pole `3.5 mm` push-in spring terminal (`J1`: `KF250-3.5-4P-1`, `C475156` — `1: VIN+ Red`, `2: GND Black`, `3: RS485_A+ White`, `4: RS485_B- Yellow`), protected by a `1.1 A` / `60 V` `2920` PTC fuse, `SMAJ36A` TVS diode (`58.1 V` clamp), and `SS16` reverse-polarity Schottky diode (every VIN part rated ≥ `60 V`), with the separate `power` board (2× `TPS54360` 60 V buck at `743 kHz`: `+5V` electronics, `+12V_HEAT` heaters + transducer drive, regulated above ≈ 13.5 V input and passing the input through minus ≈ 1 V below that; no `12–32 V` conductor leaves the base) + `LM66100` ideal diode feeding a socketed **Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6**.
  - **Boosted Wi-Fi (`+20.5 dBm` + External U.FL Dipole):** Firmware drives `XIAO ESP32-C6` internal `GPIO3 = LOW` and `GPIO14 = HIGH` to switch the onboard `FM8625H` RF switch to a horizontal `2.4 / 5.8 GHz` U.FL FPC dipole antenna mounted inside `base.stl`.
  - **RS-485 (`115,200 bps`):** `SP3485EN-L/TR` (`U7`) on `UART1` TX `GPIO16` / RX `GPIO17` / RTS→DE+/RE `GPIO19` (`UART_MODE_RS485_HALF_DUPLEX`), `390 Ω` fail-safe bias, optional hand-fit `120 Ω` terminator `R26`, `SM712` surge TVS (`D4`), poll / response only: `STATE?` returns the latest CRC-16-protected `$AM,...*<CRC16>` telemetry frame, plus `PING`, `RESET_RAIN`, `HEAT_CHECK` (one command per line; the station never transmits unsolicited). Multi-drop: `rs485_address` 1–247 → commands `<addr>:CMD`, replies `$AM<addr>,...`; 0 answers bare commands.
- **6-Board Modular PCB Stack (one `140.6 × 63.2 mm` breakaway panel from `panelize.pack_columns`: smallest-area column packing over rotations, column / stack order and 180° turns chosen so every board gets part-free tabs; no own rails — order with JLCPCB-added rails):**
  1. **`main` (`32.0 × 52.0 mm`, right half of the base bay):** Power input, `XIAO ESP32-C6`, `SP3485EN` RS-485, `LSM6DS3TR-C` 6-axis IMU, `AO3400A` de-icing heater MOSFET, `ADS1115` heater power monitor (shunts for both heaters). XIAO `GPIO2` / `GPIO18` / `GPIO20` = `CROWN_BOOT0` / `CROWN_NRST` (open-drain) / `CROWN_IRQ`.
  2. **`crown` v2 (`60 × 60 mm`, N/E/S/W notches for the transducer barrels, NE corner chamfered to 26.9 mm for the cable column; outline in `layout_crown.yaml`):** `STM32G431RBT6` (`U1`, I²C peripheral `0x2A`, protocol `shared/include/crown_protocol.hpp`, firmware `crown/`), 4× `UCC27524` H-bridge transducer drivers on `+12V_HEAT` (≈ 21.8 Vpp) with `BAV99` anti-parallel T/R isolation, AC-coupled receive (47k + 10 nF + 1M to VREF) → `74HC4052` (3.3 V) → `TLV9062` (G 7.8) → STM32 PGA ×2..32 → ADC 2 MSPS, piezo charge amplifier (`J6..J9`) → ADC2, `AS3935` + `MA5532-AE` antenna on SPI1 (no copper under the antenna), `MMC5603NJ` compass, `XC6206` 3.3 V LDO, dome socket `J10`, SWD debug port `J12` (Tag-Connect TC2030-NL footprint; production flashing goes through the station: BOOT0 + ROM I²C bootloader).
  3. **`reflector` (`24.0 × 24.0 mm`):** Horizontal T-cap heater board beneath the ultrasonic reflector cone with 4× `120 Ω` `2512` `2 W` parallel resistors on `+12V_HEAT` (`30 Ω`, `4.5 W` regulated), center `10k` NTC (`TH1`), and bottom 4-pin SMD male header `J1`.
  4. **`dome` (`Ø 13.6 mm` round):** Elevated UV/solar/NIR micro-PCB on a 4-pin vertical B2B riser, `0.5 mm` under a stock `Ø 20 × 2 mm` `JGS1` fused-silica window held in the crown lid's raised boss (`build_enclosure.UV_WIN`), carrying `LTR-390UV-01` (`U1`, UV + `157k lx` `ALS`), `TSL25911FN` (`U2`, `LCSC C176812`, `Visible + Near-IR 700–1100 nm` + `188 µLux` night sky), and `GUVB-C31SM` (`U3`, `AlGaN` `UV-B 240–320 nm`, populated by default, primary UV-index source) on `F.Cu` and `C1, C2, C3, J1` on `B.Cu`.
  5. **`probe` (`14.0 × 56.0 mm`):** Vertical pagoda stem carrying `SCD41-D-R2` ($\text{CO}_2$) + backside `+8 °C` thermostatic heater (`2× 680 Ω 1206`, `0.42 W` at 12 V, on `+12V_HEAT`, + `LM2903` on `+12V_HEAT` so its inputs stay inside the common-mode range, `~1.4 K` hysteresis + `TH1`), `SGP41-D-R4` ($\text{VOC}/\text{NO}_x$) + `BMP585` barometer (thunder pressure waves) above it, milled thermal isolation slot, and `SHT40-AD1F-R2` (`Temp/RH`) at the lower tip.
  6. **`power` (`28.5 × 21.5 mm`, flat under `main`):** 2× `TPS54360` (power stages on top, small-signal parts on the bottom) → `+5V` (electronics) and `+12V_HEAT` (reflector + probe heaters, crown transducer drivers + spare `J11`), + `AP2112K-3.3` LDO → `+3V3_PROBE` (dedicated probe-sensor rail, passed straight through main to probe `J1` pin 7); 10-pin JST-SH `J1` ↔ `main J5`.
- **13-Part Modular 3D-Printed Enclosure with True Meshing Threads (`hardware/releases/v1/enclosure/`):**
  - `base` (with the `PMSA003I` duct bed), `mast_clamp`, `orings`, `probe_sleeve_back`, `probe_sleeve_front`, `pagoda`, `wind_tunnel`, `struts`, `crown_base`, `crown`, `mast_shims` and `transducer_adapters` (retaining rings for the default Ø 14 × 9 mm CUSA-TR65-065-2200-TH68 transducers + optional Ø 10 mm sleeves) (`.stl` and `.step` in assembled position + individual and whole-enclosure VTK renders in `renders/`).
  - `enclosure/print/<plate>.stl` (8 print plates: `base`, `orings`, `pagoda`, `wind_tunnel`, `probe_sleeve` (front + back), `mast_clamp` (+ `mast_shims`), `struts` (+ `transducer_adapters`), `crown` (+ `crown_dome` + `crown_base`); `PRINT_GROUPS` in `build_enclosure.py`): every body laid out on the print bed `≥ 6 mm` apart (captive umbrella and reset button, louver plates, strut halves, nuts, shims). Always print/order these, never the assembled multi-body STLs (0.1–0.4 mm gaps fuse in MJF/SLS).
  - Bayonet seals are `88 × 2.0 mm` O-rings (glands sized for `2.0 mm`); pagoda bolts are DIN 7984 low-head `M6 × 55` (an ISO 4762 head hits the reflector board); the mast clamp has `2.0 mm` travel (`Ø 32–34 mm` direct, 4 `mast_shims` sizes for `Ø 24–32 mm`).

## Repository layout and verification commands

- `station/`: ESP32-C6 C++17 firmware (PlatformIO / ESP-IDF 5.5), LittleFS Web UI, MQTT Home Assistant Auto-Discovery, RS-485 protocol driver, and native Unity unit tests.
- `hardware/`: SKiDL circuit generators (`circuits/`), KiCad 10 PCB layouts (`layout/v1/`), JLCPCB/EasyEDA pin-for-pin verification (`scripts/jlc_parts.py`), breakaway panelizer (`scripts/panelize.py`: column packing, tabs placed clear of parts), and manufacturing package (`releases/v1/`).
- `crown/`: crown STM32G431 firmware (PlatformIO `stm32cube`; DSP / register bank in `lib/crown_core`, host-tested).
- `shared/include/crown_protocol.hpp`: crown ↔ station I²C register protocol used by both firmwares.
- `hardware/sim/`: ngspice models (`crown_us_link.py`: transducer drive / T-R isolation / receive chain; asserted by `hardware/tests/test_crown_ultrasonic_sim.py`).
- `mechanical/`: Parametric CadQuery 12-part 3D roof station assembly (`scripts/build_enclosure.py`) and VTK ray-traced 3D renders (`scripts/render_assembly3d.py`).

### Commands

- Full hardware + CAD + release + test pipeline: `python3 hardware/scripts/build.py`
- Firmware native unit tests: `docker compose --profile lint run --rm -T cpp-lint "pio test -d station -e native"`
- Lint (Python & C++): `docker compose --profile lint run --rm lint check .`
- Crown firmware: `docker compose --profile lint run --rm -T cpp-lint "pio test -d crown -e native"` (host tests) and `"pio run -d crown -e crown"` (STM32 build; GCC 12 pinned because the arm64 container has no GCC 7 build).
- ESP32-C6 firmware build (compile check incl. drivers): `docker compose --profile lint run --rm -T cpp-lint "cd station && pio run -e dev_c6"` (native tests do NOT compile `src/firmware/`).
- Web UI dev server: `python3 station/scripts/mock_webui_server.py` (`http://127.0.0.1:8765`). It auto-builds `station/build/station_sim` (host build of the real `src/core` pipeline, needs `c++` and the ArduinoJson copy in `station/.pio/libdeps/native`, i.e. run the native tests once) and serves the same API as the firmware. Scenarios: `POST /api/action {"action":"set_scenario","scenario":"<name>"}` with `breeze`, `storm`, `freeze`, `heatwave`, `plume`, `train`, `night_dark`, `night_moon` (unknown keys are ignored). Scenario raw-sensor models live in `station/tools/sim/station_sim.cpp`. Repack firmware assets after UI edits: `python3 station/scripts/pack_webui.py`.
- Web UI runtime smoke test: `node` is not installed locally. Run `station/data/app.js` with macOS JXA (`osascript -l JavaScript`) using a stub `document`/`fetch` harness and feed `/api/state` JSON into `applyTelemetry(d)` for each scenario. `applyTelemetry` errors are swallowed by `.catch(() => {})` in the browser, so a throw shows up as cards that silently stop updating. JXA predefines a global `$`, so `app.js` must not declare one (its DOM helper is `el`).

## Firmware data architecture

- Drivers (`src/firmware/drivers/`) + host-tested conditioners (`src/conditioning/`) write corrected 1-s values into `core::RawState` (`Reading<T>` = value + timestamp + valid) and discrete events (rain impacts, lightning, thunder onsets) into `core::EventQueues`.
- `core::Pipeline::step()` runs once per second: processors read a RawState copy, drain events, and fully rewrite `core::DerivedState` (`Value<T>`; `ok == false` is published as JSON `null`, shown as "—"). Stale/missing sensors never produce defaults.
- `history::TieredHistory`: 27 metrics × (60 × 1 min + 24 × 1 h + 31 × 1 day), mean/min/max/sum/count/vector-direction slots, ~50 KB RAM; hour/day tiers persist to SPIFFS hourly.
- Publishers read DerivedState/history only: `/api/state` + WebSocket + MQTT state (current values only; Home Assistant keeps long-term history), `/api/history/minutes` (60-bar card charts), `/api/history?tier=minute|hour|day&metrics=a,b`, `/api/config`, `/api/action` (`reset_rain`, `set_time`, `reboot`), RS-485 `$AM` frames.
- The Web UI is display-only: no metric is computed in `app.js`.
- Flash: the XIAO ESP32-C6 has **4 MB**. `partitions.csv` = 2 × 1.75 MB OTA app slots + 384 KB LittleFS `storage` (history, `/data`); `sdkconfig.defaults` and `platformio.ini` (`board_upload.flash_size`) must stay at 4 MB.
- The Web UI is embedded in the firmware as gzip (`WebAssets.hpp`, generated by `scripts/pack_webui.py`; never edit it by hand). Edit `station/data/` instead.
- UV index: GUVB-C31SM (fitted by default, factory NVM offset/B_Scale or `guvb_counts_per_uvi`) fused 80/20 with LTR-390; both per-sensor values are published.
- Air: PMSA003I (PM1/2.5/10, no PM4) lying face-down on the left half of the `base` bay on two sealed duct chambers (geometry in `enclosure_features.PM`, tested by `test_pm_bed_ducts_take_outside_air_only`); `main` sits on the right half (`x_offset` 15.5 in `spec.yaml`), I2C 0x12 at 100 kHz, 30 s fan warm-up. VOC/NOx: SGP41 raw ticks through Sensirion Gas Index Algorithm 3.2.3 (`src/airquality/GasIndex.*`); the library has Arduino metadata, hence `lib_compat_mode = off`, and must be included via `<SensirionGasIndexAlgorithm.h>` so PlatformIO's LDF links it.
- Barometer: BMP585 on the probe (`U5`, same register map as BMP581, chip ID 0x51, I2C 0x47).
- New JLC parts: fetch the supplier data (`hardware/build/jlc/<LCSC>.{jlc,easyeda}.json`), then `python3 hardware/scripts/jlc_parts.py snapshot --cached` in the `eda` container; footprints must land pin-for-pin on the EasyEDA pads.
- Heaters: both on +12V_HEAT from power-board U2 (separate TPS54360; electronics on +5V from U1): reflector (4x 120 ohm = 30 ohm, 4.5 W firmware constant-wattage PWM on the measured rail, NTC, UVLO 9 V, open-NTC `SensorFault` lockout) and probe (analog +8 C thermostat). Probe cable = +12V_HEAT x2, HEAT_SW x2, GND, NTC_SENSE, +3V3, CO2_HEAT_RTN, SDA, SCL. Crown cable (12-pin) = +12V_HEAT x2, GND x2, +5V, GND, SDA, GND, SCL, CROWN_IRQ, CROWN_NRST, CROWN_BOOT0. ADS1115 AIN2 = +12V_HEAT/4 (30k/10k), AIN3 = +5V/2. Both currents are measured by the ADS1115 on main (I2C 0x48, AIN0 reflector 0.1 ohm, AIN1 probe 1 ohm via the CO2_HEAT_RTN cable wire, AIN2 +5V/2); the probe heater zone temperature is the SCD41 on-chip temperature. `HEAT_CHECK` (RS-485) or the `heater_check` HTTP action runs a 10 s 2 W reflector pulse and reports check-pass/check-fail in `heater.reflector_status`.
- Removing auto-referenced parts renumbers SKiDL's references (R/C), and parts with explicit refs created early shift later auto refs: create explicit-ref parts last, or remap the layout keys from the old/new `parts.json` (source + value + nets).
- Heights: the base bay is 15 mm taller (`envelope.above_base_raise` in spec.yaml). Parts above the base (pagoda, probe, reflector, struts, crown, dome) keep their design-frame coordinates in spec + `build_enclosure.py` and are lifted by `ef.RAISE` (builders wrap `_build_*().translate`, `ef.board_point` adds it for non-base boards); `main`/`power` y in spec are absolute. Inside the bay: power board top 41.7, main top 56.5 (reset pin +`BTN_UP` 10), umbrella +`UMB` 12, PM face 45.5.
- Layout plan extras (`layout_<board>.yaml` frame): `copper_keepout` entries accept an optional `layers: [In1.Cu, ...]` list (default all copper; used for the AS3935 antenna keepout on the crown), and `power_track_width` sets the board's power-netclass track width (default 0.3 mm; power board 0.5 mm).
- Design review: open items and acceptance checks live in `REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`; run the `/manufacturing-review` skill (`.claude/skills/manufacturing-review/`) for an independent pre-order review. Geometry checks must use real ISO fastener / O-ring / cable sizes, not the modelled placeholders.
- JLC part search (no LCSC number yet): POST `{"keyword": ..., "currentPage": 1, "pageSize": 6}` to `https://jlcpcb.com/api/overseas-pcb-order/v1/shoppingCart/smtGood/selectSmtComponentList` (`data.componentPageInfo.list[]`: `componentCode`, `componentModelEn`, `stockCount`, `componentLibraryType`).
- Vertical stack: `envelope.above_pagoda_raise` (6 mm) lifts the wind_tunnel deck, struts, crown_base, crown and the reflector/crown/dome boards on top of `above_base_raise` (`ef.UPPER` = both); the wind_tunnel bayonet skirt stays on the pagoda joint and is 6 mm taller (10.4 mm crown-cable gap above the pagoda deck); the probe board is 56 mm long. Hard-coded design-frame y values for those parts take `+ ef.UPPER`, pagoda/probe-sleeve ones `+ ef.RAISE`.
- Mast clamp is 40 mm tall from the base underside: `mast_socket.y_start` = -12 (31 mm pole engagement up to the pole stop at y = 19, 3 rows of M6 clamp bolts at 13 mm pitch, 31 mm tall `mast_shims`).
- Silkscreen: `layout_<board>.yaml` frame `connector_labels: {J2: "CROWN", ...}` places the destination name next to each connector (same placement search as the references, 0.7 mm text, `SILK_TEXT_MM` in `layout.py`). Main J1 is rotated 180 deg: its per-pin labels read B-, A+, GND, +V from left to right.
- Pours listed in a layout frame (`pours:`; `priority: 1` for SW / VIN / VOUT copper on the power board) also make the router's redundant tracks/vias of those nets get pruned after routing (`prune_ground_tracks`).
- Crown wind / rain / lightning: the ESP32 reads the crown over I²C (`firmware/drivers/CrownLink`, host-tested decoding in `src/crown/`), applies `us_delay_us` and the zero-wind offsets, then solves wind with `wind::UltrasonicWindSolver`. `POST /api/crown/firmware` (raw .bin) reflashes the crown through the STM32 ROM I²C bootloader (`src/crown/StmI2cBootloader`, AN4221). The ESP32 has no direct ultrasonic / rain / AS3935 drivers any more.
- Board-only parts (test / programming pads): `src(..., board_only=True)` → `Assembly = none`; skipped by sourcing, JLC pin checks, BOM and CPL.
- Commissioning / calibration of every built station: `CALIBRATION.md` (rails, crown flash, RS-485, still-air wind calibration via the `wind_calibrate` action, `wind_speed_gain`, `rain_impact_scale` / `rain_gain`, UV, lightning interference via `crown_bursts_off/on`). Crown diagnostics are in `/api/config` → `crown`.
- `StationConfig` is stored as an NVS blob: only ever APPEND fields (a shorter blob from older firmware then still loads).
- Crown firmware is embedded in the station firmware (`station/src/firmware/CrownImage.hpp`): after changing `crown/`, bump `kVersion` in `crown/src/main.cpp`, build it (`pio run -d crown -e crown`) and run `python3 station/scripts/pack_crown_image.py`; the station reflashes a crown with another version (or a blank one) at boot (`/api/config` → `crown.auto_update`).
- Enclosure guard tests: radiation-labyrinth ray test, swept crown-cable / probe-jumper clearance (`_cable_tube`, Ø 3.2 mm, 10 mm bend), printed walls ≥ `MIN_WALL_MM` 0.75 mm (ray-cast median thickness). Electrical rules (cold rating of every part, cross-domain logic levels, comparator inputs): `hardware/tests/test_electrical_rules.py`.
- UV covers: two crown tops on the `crown` print plate: `crown` (stock Ø 20 × 2 mm JGS1 window, `UV_WIN` / `UV_BOSS`, 17 × 1.0 O-ring between window top and lip) and `crown_dome` (Ø 20 mm hollow JGS1 hemisphere, `UV_DOME`, flush collar below the sensor tops for a horizon view, 19 × 1.0 O-ring in the bore / lip corner gland). The O-ring always seals lid ↔ glass; both M22 nuts (`uv_retainer_nut`) print on the `struts` plate. Tests: `test_uv_window_stack_fits_the_crown_lid`, `test_uv_dome_crown_top_fits`. All PCBs are 1.6 mm (probe sleeve slot ±0.9 mm).
