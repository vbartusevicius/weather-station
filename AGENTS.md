# Roof Weather Station (AeroMet V1)

## Project intent and current scope

Design and build a solid-state, cylindrical rooftop weather and air-quality station (`Ø 96 × 235 mm` in UV-stabilized ASA) with full end-to-end automated hardware, mechanical, and firmware verification:

- **Power & Dual-Path Connectivity (`main` PCB):**
  - Wide-input **12–32 V DC wired supply + RS-485** on a single 4-pole `3.5 mm` push-in spring terminal (`J1`: `KF250-3.5-4P-1`, `C475156` — `1: VIN+ Red`, `2: GND Black`, `3: RS485_A+ White`, `4: RS485_B- Yellow`), protected by a `1.1 A` / `60 V` `2920` PTC fuse, `SMAJ36A` TVS diode (`58.1 V` clamp), and `SS16` reverse-polarity Schottky diode (every VIN part rated ≥ `60 V`), with the separate `power` board (2× `TPS54360` 60 V buck at `743 kHz`: `+5V` electronics, `+12V_HEAT` heaters + transducer drive, regulated above ≈ 13.5 V input and passing the input through minus ≈ 1 V below that; no `12–32 V` conductor leaves the base) + `LM66100` ideal diode feeding a socketed **Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6**.
  - **Boosted Wi-Fi (`+20.5 dBm` + External U.FL Dipole):** Firmware drives `XIAO ESP32-C6` internal `GPIO3 = LOW` and `GPIO14 = HIGH` to switch the onboard `FM8625H` RF switch to a horizontal `2.4 GHz` U.FL FPC dipole (the ESP32-C6 has no 5 GHz radio) antenna mounted inside `base.stl`.
  - **RS-485 (`115,200 bps`):** `SP3485EN-L/TR` (`U7`) on `UART1` TX `GPIO16` / RX `GPIO17` / RTS→DE+/RE `GPIO19` (`UART_MODE_RS485_HALF_DUPLEX`), `390 Ω` fail-safe bias, optional hand-fit `120 Ω` terminator `R26`, `SM712` surge TVS (`D4`), poll / response only: `STATE?` returns the latest CRC-16-protected `$AM,...*<CRC16>` telemetry frame, plus `PING`, `RESET_RAIN`, `HEAT_CHECK` (one command per line; the station never transmits unsolicited). Multi-drop: `rs485_address` 1–247 → commands `<addr>:CMD`, replies `$AM<addr>,...`; 0 answers bare commands.
- **6-Board Modular PCB Stack (one `140.6 × 63.2 mm` breakaway panel from `panelize.pack_columns`: smallest-area column packing over rotations, column / stack order and 180° turns chosen so every board gets part-free tabs; no own rails — order with JLCPCB-added rails):**
  1. **`main` (`32.0 × 52.0 mm`, right half of the base bay):** Power input, `XIAO ESP32-C6`, `SP3485EN` RS-485, `LSM6DS3TR-C` 6-axis IMU, `AO3400A` de-icing heater MOSFET, `ADS1115` heater power monitor (shunts for both heaters). XIAO `GPIO2` / `GPIO18` / `GPIO20` = `CROWN_BOOT0` / `CROWN_NRST` (open-drain) / `CROWN_IRQ`.
  2. **`crown` v2 (`60 × 60 mm`, N/E/S/W notches for the transducer barrels, NE corner chamfered to 26.9 mm for the cable column; outline in `layout_crown.yaml`):** `STM32G431RBT6` (`U1`, I²C peripheral `0x2A`, protocol `shared/include/crown_protocol.hpp`, firmware `crown/`), 4× `UCC27524` H-bridge transducer drivers on `+12V_HEAT` (≈ 21.8 Vpp) with `BAV99` anti-parallel T/R isolation, AC-coupled receive (47k + 10 nF + 1M to VREF) → `74HC4052` (3.3 V) → `TLV9062` (G 7.8, output idles at VREF) → STM32 OPAMP1 PGA ×2..32 in the "inverting input as bias" mode with VREF on PA3 / VINM0 (the plain PGA mode would multiply the 1.65 V bias into the rail) → ADC 2 MSPS, piezo charge amplifier (`J6..J9`) → 22k / 1 nF (7.2 kHz) → ADC2 (rain detector blanked during each burst + 1 ms), ROM-bootloader pull-ups on PA10 / PB12 (R28 / R29), `AS3935` + `MA5532-AE` antenna on SPI1 (no copper under the antenna), `MMC5603NJ` compass, `XC6206` 3.3 V LDO, dome socket `J10`, SWD debug port `J12` (Tag-Connect TC2030-NL footprint; production flashing goes through the station: BOOT0 + ROM I²C bootloader).
  3. **`reflector` (`24.0 × 24.0 mm`):** Horizontal T-cap heater board beneath the ultrasonic reflector cone with 4× `120 Ω` `2512` `2 W` parallel resistors on `+12V_HEAT` (`30 Ω`, `4.5 W` regulated), center `10k` NTC (`TH1`), and bottom 4-pin SMD male header `J1`.
  4. **`dome` (`Ø 13.6 mm` round):** Elevated UV/solar/NIR micro-PCB on a 4-pin vertical B2B riser, under the default turned PTFE dome (`build_enclosure.UV_PTFE`: flat-faced Ø 20.4 × 1.5 mm flange, 18 × 1.0 O-ring squeezed flat against the collar lip, dome nut under the flange; shapes `UV_PTFE_LATHE` = cylinder + flat top from round rod (default) and `UV_PTFE_CNC` = hemisphere; low flush collar: horizon view) or the backup stock `Ø 20 × 2 mm` `JGS1` window in the `crown_window` boss (`UV_WIN`), carrying `LTR-390UV-01` (`U1`, UV + `157k lx` `ALS`), `TSL25911FN` (`U2`, `LCSC C176812`, `Visible + Near-IR 700–1100 nm` + `188 µLux` night sky), and `GUVB-C31SM` (`U3`, `AlGaN` `UV-B 240–320 nm`, populated by default, primary UV-index source) on `F.Cu` and `C1, C2, C3, J1` on `B.Cu`.
  5. **`probe` (`14.0 × 56.0 mm`):** Vertical pagoda stem, bottom to top: `J1`, milled slot, `SHT40-AD1F-R2` (`Temp/RH`), milled slot (In1 ground plane cut in its side bridges), `SGP41-D-R4` ($\text{VOC}/\text{NO}_x$) + `BMP585` barometer (thunder pressure waves), `SCD41-D-R2` ($\text{CO}_2$) + backside `−7.6 °C` thermostatic heater (`2× 680 Ω 1206`, `0.42 W` at 12 V, on `+12V_HEAT`, + `LM2903` on `+12V_HEAT` so its inputs stay inside the common-mode range, `~2.3 K` hysteresis + `TH1`; the SCD41 is rated −10 °C and the heater stays off above −8 °C so it cannot bias the SHT40), `J2` to the reflector.
  6. **`power` (`28.5 × 21.5 mm`, flat under `main`):** 2× `TPS54360` (power stages on top, small-signal parts on the bottom) → `+5V` (electronics) and `+12V_HEAT` (reflector + probe heaters, crown transducer drivers + spare `J11`), + `AP2112K-3.3` LDO → `+3V3_PROBE` (dedicated probe-sensor rail, passed straight through main to probe `J1` pin 7); 10-pin JST-SH `J1` ↔ `main J5`.
- **13-Part Modular 3D-Printed Enclosure with True Meshing Threads (`hardware/releases/v1/enclosure/`):**
  - `base` (with the `PMSA003I` duct bed), `mast_clamp`, `orings`, `probe_sleeve_back`, `probe_sleeve_front`, `pagoda`, `wind_tunnel`, `struts`, `crown_base`, `crown`, `mast_shims` and `transducer_adapters` (retaining rings for the default Ø 14 × 9 mm CUSA-TR65-065-2200-TH68 transducers + optional Ø 10 mm sleeves) (`.stl` and `.step` in assembled position + individual and whole-enclosure VTK renders in `renders/`).
  - `enclosure/print/<plate>.stl` (8 print plates: `base`, `orings`, `pagoda`, `wind_tunnel`, `probe_sleeve` (front + back), `mast_clamp` (+ `mast_shims`), `struts` (+ `transducer_adapters`), `crown` (+ `crown_window` + `crown_base`); `PRINT_GROUPS` in `build_enclosure.py`): every body laid out on the print bed `≥ 6 mm` apart (captive umbrella and reset button, louver plates, strut halves, nuts, shims). Always print/order these, never the assembled multi-body STLs (0.1–0.4 mm gaps fuse in MJF/SLS).
  - Bayonet seals are `88 × 2.0 mm` O-rings (glands sized for `2.0 mm`); pagoda bolts are DIN 7984 low-head `M6 × 55` (an ISO 4762 head hits the reflector board); the mast clamp has `2.0 mm` travel (`Ø 32–34 mm` direct, 4 `mast_shims` sizes for `Ø 24–32 mm`).

Keep `README.md` short: overview, small galleries, collapsed extra renders, links into `docs/`.
- `hardware/sim/`: ngspice models (`crown_us_link.py`: transducer drive / T-R isolation / receive chain; asserted by `hardware/tests/test_crown_ultrasonic_sim.py`).
- `mechanical/`: Parametric CadQuery 13-part 3D roof station assembly (`scripts/build_enclosure.py`) and VTK ray-traced 3D renders (`scripts/render_assembly3d.py`).

- ESP32-C6 firmware build (compile check incl. drivers): `docker compose --profile lint run --rm -T cpp-lint "cd station && pio run -e dev_c6"` (native tests do NOT compile `src/firmware/`).
- Web UI dev server: `python3 station/scripts/mock_webui_server.py [--port N] [--product-look]` (`http://127.0.0.1:8765`; `--product-look` reports `simulated: false`, so the page looks like a station: no scenario picker, used for README screenshots). It auto-builds `station/build/station_sim` (host build of the real `src/core` pipeline, needs `c++` and the ArduinoJson copy in `station/.pio/libdeps/native`, i.e. run the native tests once) and serves the same API as the firmware. Scenarios: `POST /api/action {"action":"set_scenario","scenario":"<name>"}` with `breeze`, `storm`, `freeze`, `heatwave`, `plume`, `train`, `night_dark`, `night_moon` (unknown keys are ignored). Scenario raw-sensor models live in `station/tools/sim/station_sim.cpp`. Repack firmware assets after UI edits: `python3 station/scripts/pack_webui.py`.
- CI: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (push to main, PRs, manual) runs the same containers: lint; station native tests (+ ASan/UBSan) and the Web UI smoke test `node station/tests/webui/smoke.mjs [url]` against the simulator (every scenario, both unit systems, every Trends range); crown native tests + STM32 build; firmware release (artifact: `firmware.bin`, `usb-flash-firmware.bin`; fails if the embedded crown image changes); hardware + CAD pipeline with its tests (`build.py --eda --cad --release --test`, ~1–2 h); then `release` (needs every job; every push to `main` and manual runs; named after the UTC commit time `yyyy.mm.dd.hhmm`, the firmware version): collects the firmware artifact, zips the committed `hardware/releases/v1/` + `CALIBRATION.md` + `docs/hardware.md` + `docs/building.md` as `aeromet-hardware-v1.zip`, adds `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and creates / updates the GitHub release with `gh` (nothing is rebuilt; created as a draft, made public after the last upload). A new push to `main` or a new PR commit cancels the run in progress for it (`concurrency`: workflow + PR number / ref). Locally without node: `docker run --rm -v "$PWD:/work" -w /work node:22-alpine node station/tests/webui/smoke.mjs http://host.docker.internal:8765`.
- Web UI runtime smoke test (ad hoc): `node` is not installed locally. Run `station/data/app.js` with macOS JXA (`osascript -l JavaScript`) using a stub `document`/`fetch` harness and feed `/api/state` JSON into `applyTelemetry(d)` for each scenario. `applyTelemetry` errors are swallowed by `.catch(() => {})` in the browser, so a throw shows up as cards that silently stop updating. ## Firmware data architecture

- Drivers (`src/firmware/drivers/`) + host-tested conditioners (`src/conditioning/`) write corrected 1-s values into `core::RawState` (`Reading<T>` = value + timestamp + valid) and discrete events (rain impacts, lightning, thunder onsets) into `core::EventQueues`.
- `core::Pipeline::step()` runs once per second: processors read a RawState copy, drain events, and fully rewrite `core::DerivedState` (`Value<T>`; `ok == false` is published as JSON `null`, shown as "—"). Stale/missing sensors never produce defaults.
- History: `history::TieredHistory` keeps in RAM only the last 60 minutes, a cache of the last 24 closed hours (the pipeline reads it every second) and the running minute / hour / day (26 metrics, mean/min/max/sum/count/vector-direction slots, 32-bit sample counts, ~23 KB). Each closed hour / day (all metrics + that interval's wind-rose bucket) is appended by `main.cpp` to `history::HistoryStore`, a raw-flash record log on the `storage` partition (no file system): superblock + hourly ring (≥ 14 days) + daily ring (≥ 6 months), CRC-32 per record, torn writes skipped, a foreign / old layout is erased on first start (`HistoryStore::kSectorsNeeded` must fit the partition). `TieredHistory::long_slot` serves both (RAM for ≤ 24 h, the store beyond); `Pipeline::restore` refills the 24-h cache, today and the day's rose after a reboot. Records (highs / lows) are an NVS blob saved every closed hour. Host tests use `history::MemFlash` (NOR semantics); so does the simulator.
- Publishers read DerivedState/history only: `/api/state` + WebSocket + MQTT state (current values only; Home Assistant keeps long-term history), `/api/history/minutes` (60-bar card charts), `/api/history?tier=minute|hour|day&n=<intervals>&metrics=a,b` (text-built, no JSON document; n up to 60 / 336 / 183; sum metrics also carry a running total `cum`), `/api/history.csv` (same `tier` / `n`, local timestamps), `/api/records` and `/api/windrose?range=1h|24h|7d|14d|31d|6mo` (`history::Climate`: records today / yesterday / month / all time with times, "vs. this time yesterday" from the oldest RAM hour, and a 16-sector × 6-speed-class rose fed once per closed minute and summed from the stored hour / day buckets), `/api/config`, `/api/action` (`reset_rain`, `set_time`, `reboot`), RS-485 `$AM` frames.
- Data quality + health (`src/quality/Quality.*`, host-tested; run at the end of `Pipeline::step`): WMO-style QC flags per published measurement (`good` / `suspect` / `fail` / `missing` + reason: range, one-minute step "spike" held 10 min, persistence "stuck", and cross-checks: sunlight / UV at night, irradiance > 1.5 × clear-sky + 150 W/m², rain at RH < 40 %, sonic vs SHT40 temperature > 6 K, CO₂ < 350 ppm) in `d.quality.qc`, and a supervisor-style health list (supply, rails, crown wind / rain, each sensor, tilt, compass, dome optics, both heaters; Wi-Fi added by the publisher) in `d.quality.health`: each entry carries a status and a reason / note (empty when normal); the Web UI adds the live reading per entry (`HEALTH_VALUES` in `app.js`, unit-aware), and the Calibration header shows only sun position and the raw light behind the dome. Published as `quality`, `health` and `health_summary` in `/api/state` / MQTT (HA sensor "Station Health"); shown at the top of the Web UI **Calibration** tab (each light folds in its QC flags; reasons state the problem, not a procedure) and as badges on the Now cards. Flags never alter a value.
- Web UI tabs: Now, Trends, **Calibration** (Station health + data quality, Location & time, the home calibration steps, and "All calibration values" for manual entry) and **Setup** (Wi-Fi, MQTT & Home Assistant, RS-485 status, Maintenance). `/#<tab>` opens a tab directly. The Setup tab polls `/api/config` every 5 s for status only (`renderSetupStatus`; inputs are not overwritten).
- MQTT: `mqtt_uri` accepts a host, `host:port` or a full `mqtt://` / `mqtts://` URI; `mqtt_user` / `mqtt_pass` (write-only, `/api/config` reports `mqtt_pass_set`), `mqtt_topic` base ("" = `aeromet/<node id>`; state on `<base>/state`, also used as `stat_t` in the discovery), `ha_discovery`, `ha_prefix`, `mqtt_interval_s`; `/api/config` `mqtt` = configured / connected / state_topic / node_id. Network and MQTT changes need a reboot (`reboot_required`). A missing `wifi_pass` keeps the stored password.
- RS-485 frame: 33 fields (the original 10, then gust, 3-s wind / direction, dew point, feels like, station pressure, 3-h change, irradiance, lux, cloud cover, rain total, hail, PM1 / PM2.5 / PM10, CO₂, VOC / NOx index, strikes today, last strike km, PPV, supply V, health 0–3); keep appending, never reorder.
- RS-485 diagnostics: `rs485::BusStats` (lines, per-command counts, replies, ignored, last command) and the field list `Rs485Protocol::frame_fields()` (must match `encode_telemetry_frame`, tested) are published in `/api/config` `rs485` with the current frame; the Setup card decodes it. The CRC-16/MODBUS covers everything before `*`, including `$`.
- Health also covers lightning (crown diagnostics in `RawState::crown`: AS3935 antenna resonance 482.5–517.5 kHz, disturbers per 10 min > 50 = interference; published as `lightning.antenna_khz` / `disturbers_10min`) and vibration / seismic (accelerometer data present); `solar.lux_behind_dome` = lux × `optics_transmission`.
- Trends tab: one chart with a picker (`TREND_FAMILIES` / `TREND_PRESETS` in `app.js`): a Preset menu replaces the selection, the selected series are removable chips (they are the legend), and "+ Add metric" opens a searchable popover grouped by family (family row = add / remove the whole family, items over the 6-unit limit disabled); selection kept in `localStorage`. Records and the daily summary are collapsed `<details>` loaded only while open. The wind rose is a picker entry (`rose: true`, Wind family): drawn as a polar panel beside the lanes (or alone), over the same range (`/api/windrose?range=1h|24h|7d|14d|31d|6mo`, `1h` = the running hour); it does not count as a lane. Y scales: one lane per unit (`LANES`: same unit = same axis, stacked under one shared time axis, max 6 units), min–max band for mean metrics, bars for sums, `span` keeps near-constant data flat; drawn 1:1 in CSS pixels and redrawn on resize; night shading on the 24-h view; hover read-out of every selected series. Below: records, wind rose, 31-day summary table, CSV downloads (the chart's CSV link carries the selected metrics). Code that runs at load must come after the definitions it uses (the deep-link call is the last line of `app.js`).
- The Web UI is display-only: no metric is computed in `app.js` (the calibration section only converts its own inputs: GPS km/h or distance / time → m/s, container water grams → mm).
- `index.html` contains no readings: every value element starts as "—" (the smoke test checks a sample), so a page without station data never shows mock-up numbers. The header badge (`pill-link`) is driven by data arrival: CONNECTING → LIVE → OFFLINE after 15 s without telemetry. Failed requests / renders go to `logErr` (console), never `.catch(() => {})`.
- Firmware version: `scripts/gen_version.py` sets `STATION_FW_VERSION` from `$FW_VERSION` or the UTC commit time of HEAD, `yyyy.mm.dd.hhmm` (+ `-dirty` with uncommitted tracked changes); never hard-code it. `release_firmware.py` passes the same value to the build, and the GitHub release of that commit has the same name.
- Home calibration (`src/calibration/Calibration.*`, host-tested; CALIBRATION.md section 0; Web UI **Calibration** tab): `GET/POST /api/calibration` sessions (`compass`, `heading`, `car_run`, `drop_test`, `rain_gauge`, `pressure`, `clear_sky`, `uv_reference`, `salt_rh`, `ice_point`, `co2_fresh_air`) fed one `calibration::Sample` per second by the pipeline task (values before the correction each procedure sets) plus raw crown rain peaks; a result only changes `StationConfig` on `apply` (stamped in `cal_unix[kind]`). Corrections applied in `task_slow_i2c`: magnetometer hard / soft iron (`mag_offset_ut`, `mag_scale`), `heading_offset_deg` or `heading_mode` 1 (fixed heading: zero field + the fixed bearing in `declination_deg`), SHT40 `temp_offset_c` / `rh_offset_pct`; the SCD41 forced recalibration runs there too. The simulator answers `cal <json>` (its magnetometer turns 8°/s during a compass spin).
- Firmware update: `POST /api/firmware` (station `firmware.bin`, ESP image magic 0xE9 checked, written to the inactive OTA slot, verified by `esp_ota_end`, then reboot) ; Setup tab: one upload (drop zone + progress). The crown firmware is embedded in the station image (`CrownImage.hpp`) and installed by the station at boot whenever the crown's version differs, so a crown flashed separately (`POST /api/crown/firmware`, development only, CALIBRATION.md step 2) is replaced at the next boot. `CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_APP_ROLLBACK_ENABLE=y`: a new image is marked valid after 60 s in `task_pipeline`, otherwise the bootloader returns to the previous one (needs a bootloader flashed over USB with this option once; OTA never updates the bootloader). A changed `sdkconfig.defaults` only reaches an existing build after deleting `.pio/build/<env>/sdkconfig`.
- Flash: the XIAO ESP32-C6 has **4 MB**. `partitions.csv` = 2 × 1.75 MB OTA app slots + 384 KB `storage` data partition (raw HistoryStore log; the subtype still says `spiffs` so the table stays OTA-compatible); `sdkconfig.defaults` and `platformio.ini` (`board_upload.flash_size`) must stay at 4 MB.
- The Web UI is embedded in the firmware as gzip (`WebAssets.hpp`, generated by `scripts/pack_webui.py`; never edit it by hand). Edit `station/data/` instead.
- UV index: GUVB-C31SM (fitted by default, factory NVM offset/B_Scale or `guvb_counts_per_uvi`) fused 80/20 with LTR-390; both per-sensor values are published.
- Air: PMSA003I (PM1/2.5/10, no PM4) lying face-down on the left half of the `base` bay on two sealed duct chambers (geometry in `enclosure_features.PM`, tested by `test_pm_bed_ducts_take_outside_air_only`); `main` sits on the right half (`x_offset` 15.5 in `spec.yaml`), I2C 0x12 at 100 kHz, 30 s fan warm-up. VOC/NOx: SGP41 raw ticks through Sensirion Gas Index Algorithm 3.2.3 (`src/airquality/GasIndex.*`); the library has Arduino metadata, hence `lib_compat_mode = off`, and must be included via `<SensirionGasIndexAlgorithm.h>` so PlatformIO's LDF links it.
- Barometer: BMP585 on the probe (`U5`, same register map as BMP581, chip ID 0x51, I2C 0x47).
- New JLC parts: fetch the supplier data (`hardware/build/jlc/<LCSC>.{jlc,easyeda}.json`), then `python3 hardware/scripts/jlc_parts.py snapshot --cached` in the `eda` container; footprints must land pin-for-pin on the EasyEDA pads.
- Heaters: both on +12V_HEAT from power-board U2 (separate TPS54360; electronics on +5V from U1): reflector (4x 120 ohm = 30 ohm, 4.5 W firmware constant-wattage PWM on the measured rail, NTC, UVLO 9 V, open-NTC `SensorFault` lockout) and probe (analog −7.6 C thermostat). Probe cable = +12V_HEAT x2, HEAT_SW x2, GND, NTC_SENSE, +3V3, CO2_HEAT_RTN, SDA, SCL. Crown cable (12-pin) = +12V_HEAT x2, GND x2, +5V, GND, SDA, GND, SCL, CROWN_IRQ, CROWN_NRST, CROWN_BOOT0. Both currents are measured by the ADS1115 on main (I2C 0x48, AIN0 reflector 0.1 ohm, AIN1 probe 1 ohm via the CO2_HEAT_RTN cable wire, AIN2 = +12V_HEAT/4 (30k/10k), AIN3 = +5V/2); the probe heater zone temperature is the SCD41 on-chip temperature. `HEAT_CHECK` (RS-485) or the `heater_check` HTTP action runs a 10 s 2 W reflector pulse and reports check-pass/check-fail in `heater.reflector_status`.
- Removing auto-referenced parts renumbers SKiDL's references (R/C), and parts with explicit refs created early shift later auto refs: create explicit-ref parts last, or remap the layout keys from the old/new `parts.json` (source + value + nets).
- Heights: the base bay is 15 mm taller (`envelope.above_base_raise` in spec.yaml). Parts above the base (pagoda, probe, reflector, struts, crown, dome) keep their design-frame coordinates in spec + `build_enclosure.py` and are lifted by `ef.RAISE` (builders wrap `_build_*().translate`, `ef.board_point` adds it for non-base boards); `main`/`power` y in spec are absolute. Inside the bay: power board top 41.7, main top 56.5 (reset pin +`BTN_UP` 10), umbrella +`UMB` 12, PM face 45.5.
- Layout plan extras (`layout_<board>.yaml` frame): `copper_keepout` entries accept an optional `layers: [In1.Cu, ...]` list (default all copper; used for the AS3935 antenna keepout on the crown), and `power_track_width` sets the board's power-netclass track width (default 0.3 mm; power board 0.5 mm).
- Design review: open items and acceptance checks live in `REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`; run the `/manufacturing-review` skill (`.claude/skills/manufacturing-review/`) for an independent pre-order review. Geometry checks must use real ISO fastener / O-ring / cable sizes, not the modelled placeholders.
- JLC part search (no LCSC number yet): POST `{"keyword": ..., "currentPage": 1, "pageSize": 6}` to `https://jlcpcb.com/api/overseas-pcb-order/v1/shoppingCart/smtGood/selectSmtComponentList` (`data.componentPageInfo.list[]`: `componentCode`, `componentModelEn`, `stockCount`, `componentLibraryType`).
- Vertical stack: `envelope.above_pagoda_raise` (6 mm) lifts the wind_tunnel deck, struts, crown_base, crown and the reflector/crown/dome boards on top of `above_base_raise` (`ef.UPPER` = both); the wind_tunnel bayonet skirt stays on the pagoda joint and is 6 mm taller (10.4 mm crown-cable gap above the pagoda deck); the probe board is 56 mm long. Hard-coded design-frame y values for those parts take `+ ef.UPPER`, pagoda/probe-sleeve ones `+ ef.RAISE`.
- Mast clamp is 40 mm tall from the base underside: `mast_socket.y_start` = -12 (31 mm pole engagement up to the pole stop at y = 19, 3 rows of M6 clamp bolts at 13 mm pitch, 31 mm tall `mast_shims`).
- Silkscreen: frame `silk_labels` accept an optional `size` (mm; stroke 15 %) and frame `silk_lines` draws polylines (`{points: [[x, y], ...], width, layer}`); the crown's NE cut corner carries the large `N ↑` orientation marker used by the calibration diagrams. `layout_<board>.yaml` frame `connector_labels: {J2: "CROWN", ...}` places the destination name next to each connector (same placement search as the references, 0.7 mm text, `SILK_TEXT_MM` in `layout.py`). Main J1 is rotated 180 deg: its per-pin labels read B-, A+, GND, +V from left to right.
- Pours listed in a layout frame (`pours:`; `priority: 1` for SW / VIN / VOUT copper on the power board) also make the router's redundant tracks/vias of those nets get pruned after routing (`prune_ground_tracks`).
- Crown wind / rain / lightning: the ESP32 reads the crown over I²C (`firmware/drivers/CrownLink`, host-tested decoding in `src/crown/`), applies `us_delay_us` and the zero-wind offsets, then solves wind with `wind::UltrasonicWindSolver`. `POST /api/crown/firmware` (raw .bin) reflashes the crown through the STM32 ROM I²C bootloader (`src/crown/StmI2cBootloader`, AN4221). The ESP32 has no direct ultrasonic / rain / AS3935 drivers any more.
- Board-only parts (test / programming pads): `src(..., board_only=True)` → `Assembly = none`; skipped by sourcing, JLC pin checks, BOM and CPL.
- Commissioning / calibration of every built station: `CALIBRATION.md` (rails, crown flash, RS-485, still-air wind calibration via the `wind_calibrate` action, `wind_speed_gain`, `rain_impact_scale` / `rain_gain`, UV, lightning interference via `crown_bursts_off/on`). Crown diagnostics are in `/api/config` → `crown`.
- `StationConfig` is stored as an NVS blob: only ever APPEND fields (a shorter blob from older firmware then still loads).
- Crown firmware is embedded in the station firmware (`station/src/firmware/CrownImage.hpp`): after changing `crown/`, bump `kVersion` in `crown/src/main.cpp`, build it (`pio run -d crown -e crown`) and run `python3 station/scripts/pack_crown_image.py`; the station reflashes a crown with another version (or a blank one) at boot (`/api/config` → `crown.auto_update`).
- Enclosure guard tests: radiation-labyrinth ray test, swept crown-cable / probe-jumper clearance (`_cable_tube`, Ø 3.2 mm, 10 mm bend), printed walls ≥ `MIN_WALL_MM` 0.75 mm (ray-cast median thickness). Electrical rules (cold rating of every part, cross-domain logic levels, comparator inputs): `hardware/tests/test_electrical_rules.py`.
- UV covers: two crown tops on the `crown` print plate: `crown` (default: flush collar `UV_DOME`, 0.9 mm lip over an 18 × 1.0 O-ring (`UV_DOME_ORING`) squeezed 25 % flat on the PTFE dome flange top, lid apex trimmed to `top_y` 1.0 mm under the sensor tops so the lid stays ≥ 3° below the sensors' horizon; cover = turned PTFE dome `UV_PTFE` with the profile from `ptfe_dome_profile(kind)`: `lathe` (default) or `cnc`, exported as `enclosure/ptfe_dome_<kind>.step` (lathe frame) + `drawings/ptfe_dome_<kind>.svg`) and `crown_window` (backup: stock Ø 20 × 2 mm JGS1 window, `UV_WIN` / `UV_BOSS`, 17 × 1.0 O-ring between window top and lip). Both M22 nuts (`uv_dome_nut` under the flange, `uv_window_nut` beside it) print on the `struts` plate. Tests: `test_uv_dome_crown_top_fits` (both dome shapes, flat flange, O-ring squeeze / backing, −3° horizon check), `test_uv_window_stack_fits_the_crown_lid`. Firmware `optics_transmission` (StationConfig, default 0.11 for the PTFE dome, ~0.92 for JGS1) converts LTR-390 / TSL25911 / factory-scaled GUVB readings to free-sky values. All PCBs are 1.6 mm (probe sleeve slot ±0.9 mm).
- I²C runs at 100 kHz for every device (`board::i2c_speed_hz`): ~180 pF with 2.2k pull-ups is too slow for 400 kHz (checklist N11).
- Main board power nets route at 0.4 mm (`power_track_width` in `layout_main.yaml`, checklist N7). Placement / routing changes in a `layout_<board>.yaml` only take effect with `python3 hardware/scripts/build.py --eda --fresh --boards <board>` (the incremental build keeps the old placement).
